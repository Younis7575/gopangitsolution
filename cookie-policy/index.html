Cookie Policy

Every category of cookie this site sets, why, and exactly how to switch them off.

GI
Gopang IT Solution Last updated July 28, 2026

This page explains every category of cookie and similar technology used on gopangitsolution.com, what each one is for, roughly how long it lasts, and exactly how to turn it off. It sits alongside our Privacy Policy, which covers personal information more broadly.

What cookies actually are

A cookie is a small text file that a website asks your browser to store. On your next visit the browser sends it back, which is how a site can recognise a returning visitor or remember a preference. Cookies cannot execute programs, cannot read other files on your device, and cannot install software.

Related technologies do similar jobs by different means, and where we mention cookies below we include these:

  • Local storage — data stored by the browser without being sent on every request. We use it for interface preferences.
  • Pixels and tags — a tiny image or script whose loading records that a page was viewed.
  • Session identifiers — a short-lived token that keeps you signed in while you move between administrative pages.

The cookies this site uses

1. Strictly necessary

These make the site function. They cannot be switched off from within the site because the pages that use them would stop working.

2. Preference

These remember choices you have made so the site behaves consistently — for example a selected filter on our blog or news listing, or a chosen sort order. They hold interface state, not personal information, and clearing them simply resets the interface to its default.

3. Analytics

We measure how the site is used so we know which articles are worth writing more of and which pages are failing people. This includes a small first-party tracker we built ourselves, and may include Google Analytics.

What analytics records: pages viewed, referring page, approximate location derived from IP address, browser and device type, screen size, time on page, and the path taken through the site. What it does not record: your name, email address, or the contents of anything you type into a form.

4. Advertising

This site displays advertising through Google AdSense. Advertising cookies are set by Google and its advertising partners, not by us, and we do not have access to the data they collect.

The formal position, which Google requires publishers to disclose:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising from many other vendors at aboutads.info or the NAI opt-out page.
  • More detail on how Google uses data from sites that use its services is at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Opting out of personalised advertising does not remove advertising. You will still see adverts; they will be selected on general context — such as the topic of the page — rather than on an inferred profile.

Where advertising is not shown

We deliberately do not run advertising on pages that exist only to perform a function rather than to inform: our administrative area, job and proposal application forms, submission-status pages, the error page, and pages that republish third-party headlines. Those pages are also excluded from search indexing.

5. Embedded third-party content

Some pages embed content hosted elsewhere — images from an external image service, a font provider, or occasionally a video. Embedded content can set its own cookies and behaves as though you had visited that provider's site directly. We keep third-party embeds to a minimum, both for privacy and for page speed.

How to control cookies

In your browser

Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies, and block third-party cookies specifically — which is the setting that most affects advertising cookies. Blocking all cookies will break sign-in on many sites, including our administrative area, but will not prevent you reading anything on this site.

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Prevent cross-site tracking
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions
  • Android / iOS browsers: the equivalent settings under Privacy

Targeted opt-outs

  • Personalised advertising: Google Ads Settings, plus the industry opt-out pages listed above.
  • Google Analytics: the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Everything at once: private or incognito browsing discards cookies when you close the window.

Browsers also send a "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signal if you enable it. Support for these signals across the industry is inconsistent; our own first-party analytics respects them, but we cannot guarantee the behaviour of third-party networks.

Legal basis and your rights

We rely on legitimate interest for strictly necessary and preference cookies, since the site cannot function properly without them. For analytics and advertising cookies, where consent is required by the law applicable to you — for example the GDPR in the EU and EEA, the UK GDPR, or comparable regimes — that consent is the basis on which they are set, and you may withdraw it at any time using the controls above.

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access the personal information held about you, correct it, request its deletion, object to processing, or ask that it not be sold or shared for advertising purposes. To exercise any of these, contact us using the details below. Requests concerning data held by Google as an advertising vendor are best directed to Google, since we do not hold or control it.

Children

This site is intended for businesses and adult visitors. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13, and we do not target advertising at children. If you believe a child has submitted information through our forms, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

We update this page when our use of cookies changes — for example if we add or remove an analytics or advertising provider. The current version is always here, with the "last updated" date shown above. Material changes will be summarised at the top of the page for a period after the change.
