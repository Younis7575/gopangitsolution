How We Handle Website Visitor Information

Last updated: July 7, 2026

Gopang IT Solution respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit gopangitsolution.com, contact us, request a quote, apply for a job, submit a project proposal, or use any website feature.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily submit through forms, email, WhatsApp, phone calls, or other contact methods. This can include your name, email address, phone number, company name, service interest, project details, CV or application details, and any message you send to us.

We may also collect basic technical information automatically, such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, referring pages, date and time of visit, and general usage data. This information helps us keep the website secure and improve performance.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to respond to inquiries, provide quotes, manage project requests, process job or partnership applications, improve our services, maintain website security, analyze website performance, and comply with applicable legal or business requirements.

Cookies and Google AdSense

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, analytics, advertising, and security. Cookies are small files stored on your device by your browser.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads to users based on visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also learn more about advertising choices at aboutads.info.

Google and Third-Party Services

We may use Google AdSense, Google services, analytics tools, hosting providers, email services, communication tools, and other third-party service providers. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and only as needed to provide their services.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate the website or respond to your requests, when required by law, to protect our rights, or in connection with a legitimate business transfer.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no website, email, or Internet transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You can disable or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can also contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information you have provided to us, subject to legal and business record requirements.

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and general website visitors. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

External Links

Our website may include links to third-party websites, including social platforms and communication services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
