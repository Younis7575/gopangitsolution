How Your Project Journey Works

From idea to a working product, in five steps

The same path whether you book a consultation first or send the full brief — you always know what happens next.

  1. 01

    Share Your Idea

    Tell us the project you have in mind, your course, your deadline and how far you already are. A proposal, screenshot or half-built code is welcome.

  2. 02

    Book a Consultation

    Pick any open 30-minute slot, or skip straight to sending the full project brief. Both routes reach the same consultant team.

  3. 03

    Discuss Requirements

    On Google Meet we go through modules, users, data, integrations and what your university actually requires — then tell you what is realistic in your timeline.

  4. 04

    Get Project Scope & Quote

    You receive a written scope, module list, milestones and a quotation. Nothing is approved automatically, and no price is promised before we review the work.

  5. 05

    Build & Deliver

    UI/UX, development, testing, deployment guidance and documentation — with the full source, schema and build instructions handed over at the end.
